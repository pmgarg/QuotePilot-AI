## 4. Answers A to G
**A. Process integrity.** I checked the current Stage 4 test files: no xfail markers remain, and the three round-2 markers are replaced by one-line comments with the assertions in place (`test_p05`, `test_p10`, `test_p11`). I could not diff history. Both verifiers confirmed the seven round-1 removals and the lint-only edits from git. The round-2 edit was made by the same side that fixed the code, and no verifier has judged it; that is a process weakness, but I saw nothing that weakens an assertion. In round 2 the orchestrator did the right thing: after two tests failed it changed the fix, not the tests. Decisions made by agents, not a human: (1) only workflow dry runs are exempt from the kill switch (orchestrator; judged acceptable by QA and the security verifier); (2) `quote.won` removed from the triggers (orchestrator; QA accepted); (3) the two known gaps (no compensation after a failed approved step; no record for a propose-path failure of a non-approval tool) rated Low by the security agent; (4) self-approval "accepted by design" by the security agent, not a human; (5) the S4-F06 scope reduction declared by the implementer. Items 4 and 5 need a named human.

**B. Fix round 2.** Reading the code: SEC-08 now accepts only a JSON object whose `actions` is a list and falls back to the standard "not valid JSON" reply (`assistant.py:150-165`). SEC-09 filters the explanation after two markers (`workflows.py:304-312`). SEC-10 adds `assistant.session_started` and `assistant.message` rows with no text (`assistant.py:195, 243`). The three strict-xfail tests pass, QA tested 09 and 10 independently, and CI is green on the head (with the open job). That is sufficient for "plausibly fixed", not for "verified". Missing: a security re-run of the fixes (probes: provider exception, falsy and odd `args`, a service-originated error text with a canary in the support export, `actions` with 9+ items), the open CI job, and a full Stage 4 run by the implementer on the final fix (it ran four files). S4-RR1-4 and S4-RR1-5 are gaps that the pre-written tests do not cover.

**C. Re-verification sufficiency.** Security: 12 adversarial probes plus a scan (pip-audit 0, bandit 0, `npm audit --omit=dev` 0; gitleaks and semgrep not available locally, they ran in CI). QA: 10 new tests, a full API run `1953 passed, 2 skipped, 5 xfailed`, exit 0, and a full browser run `75 passed, 1 skipped`, exit 0. For this stage that is reasonable and checklist-driven, and the real defects were found. It is not enough to call races absent (timing-dependent tests), or to say anything about the hosted planner with a live model, browser-level XSS/CSP, mobile or accessibility, migration up/down, or a kill-switch race with an in-flight approval. Neither verifier looked at SoD for tool approvals as a finding; the security verifier listed it as residual risk.

**D. Scope and open items.** Block a Stage 4 PASS under the specification: S4-F06 missing parts (needs a named human: build or accept; C1), and the self-approval question (needs a named human; C2). Block VERIFIED only: the two Stage 4 verification gaps (round 2, CI job) and Stages 1–3 conditions. Backlog (Low, document): runtime budget/retry/timeout tests, compensation gap, race-test timing, browser security and accessibility checks on the four Stage 4 pages, unmetered dry runs and sessions. Not a defect by project rule: no real hosted model, e-mail, SMS or S3 provider call; billing in test mode only. These last two mean "not tested", not "works".

**E. CI.** The recorded evidence for 5c8c992: 14 of 15 check runs success, one e2e job in progress; pytest `1966 passed, 5 skipped, 5 xfailed, 1 xpassed`; Playwright `76 passed`, `1 skipped`; CodeQL success. The earlier fix-round runs went green too (`1879 passed...`, `75 passed`; the polynomial-redos alerts were fixed at `assistant.py:84`). The evidence file itself is honest about what it did not read. Open: the second e2e job and the second api job log, the unidentified `xpassed`, skips (5 vs 2), the counts of findings inside the scan jobs. A green check is not read here as proof beyond what the job summaries say.

**F.
